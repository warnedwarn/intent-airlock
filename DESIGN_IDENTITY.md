# Identity brief

## Mechanism signature

Every agreed semantic classification consumes capacity from a deterministic category quota. The primitive is cumulative: the next action sees the capacity spent by every earlier action.

## Product metaphor

An air-traffic control room. Action intents are blips, accepted classifications become flight strips, and category capacity is visible as radar rings.

## Interaction grammar

The primary viewport is observation-first. Inputs live in a low mission drawer, while the result is expressed through quota rings, a radar state, and one clearance strip. There is no repeated card grid or argument-shaped form wall.

## Voice and documentation

Short operational calls, instrument labels, and explicit telemetry. README sections read as a flight manual, not a civic docket or field notebook.

## Evidence story

One public policy response is digest-frozen. A live run must prove classification, quota consumption, submitter-only execution, and unchanged capacity under normalized replay.
